Privacy Policy for Carovio: Collage & Carousel (Android)

Application ID: in.spattnaik.carovio (Android)
Last updated: 27 October 2026
See also: Terms of Use

Carovio: Collage & Carousel ("the app") is a photo and video collage, carousel and slideshow editor made by Siddharth Pattnaik ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. In short: your photos, videos, music and projects are edited on your device and are never uploaded by the app. There is no account, no analytics and no crash reporting, and I run no servers. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID, and offers a one-time "Remove ads" purchase through Google Play.

1. Information I collect

I do not collect your photos, videos, music, project text or exported files, and the app does not send them to me or to anyone else. The app has no sign-up or sign-in, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK, and it does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Third-party services from Google process information as described in section 6. If you contact me for support, I receive your email address, your message and anything you choose to attach, such as a screenshot or the diagnostic log described in section 4. I use this correspondence only to answer your request and resolve problems. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details.

2. Your photos, videos and music

  • You choose what the app can see. Photos and videos are picked through Android's system photo picker, and music through the system file picker. The app can read only the items you select; it does not browse or scan your gallery.
  • Copies stay in the app. So that your projects keep working after the picker's access expires, each item you add is copied into the app's private storage on your device, together with smaller preview copies and thumbnails made from it.
  • All editing and rendering happens on your device. Layouts, text, stickers, filters, music mixing and video encoding are processed locally. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Exports go where you choose. Finished images and videos are saved either to your gallery (the Pictures/Carovio or Movies/Carovio folder) or to a folder you pick. Once saved there, they are ordinary files on your device, and other apps you allow to read your media can see them. Sharing them anywhere is up to you.

3. Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following in its own private app storage, which other apps cannot read. It stays on your device:

  • your projects: their layouts, text, settings and earlier saved versions used for recovery;
  • the copies of the photos, videos and music you added, and the previews and thumbnails made from them;
  • temporary files and a progress record for exports that are running or were interrupted, so that an export can be resumed or retried;
  • whether you own "Remove ads", and the identifiers of your verified Google Play purchase (including its purchase token, used so the same purchase is not processed twice);
  • when full-screen ads were last shown, so the app can keep to its limits on how often they appear;
  • your preferences, such as light or dark theme and whether you have finished the first-run screens;
  • your ad consent choices, stored on the device by Google's User Messaging Platform; and
  • a small diagnostic log (see section 4).

No cloud backup of projects. Projects, imported media, previews and the app's database are deliberately excluded from Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. If you uninstall the app, clear its storage or move to a new phone, your projects are not restored. Images and videos you have already exported to your gallery or a folder are not affected. Small app preferences may still be included in Android's own device backup if you have turned that on.

4. Diagnostic log (only if you choose to share it)

To help diagnose problems, the app records a short technical log on your device: timestamps, app events (for example "export started" or "import failed"), error codes and error types, and random internal identifiers for projects and export jobs. The log is kept in the app's private storage, is capped in size (older entries are overwritten) and is never sent automatically.

If you tap Settings → Help → Export diagnostic log, the app writes a redacted copy and opens Android's share sheet. You decide whether to send it and to whom. The exported log contains the app version, build type, the platform name ("android"), the time it was made and the log entries. It does not contain file names or paths, text you typed, image or video content, purchase tokens, your advertising ID or other device identifiers. If you send it to me, I use it only to investigate your problem.

5. Permissions the app requests, and why

  • Notifications (Android 13 and later) — requested the first time you export. Used only for the export progress notification, which shows progress and a Cancel button while an export runs in the background. If you decline, exports still work; progress is shown inside the app.
  • Foreground service and keeping the device awake — so an export can finish while you leave the app or lock the screen. Android shows the ongoing notification while it runs, and the app stops the service and releases the wake lock as soon as the export ends.
  • Storage (Android 9 and earlier only) — requested the first time you export, only on those older Android versions, so that exports can be saved to your Pictures or Movies folder. On Android 10 and later the app declares no storage permission.
  • Internet and network state — to load ads and the consent form, and to talk to Google Play for the "Remove ads" purchase. Editing and exporting work fully offline.
  • Advertising ID and related ad-services permissions — added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK so that ads can be shown and measured (see section 6).
  • Google Play billing — to make and restore the "Remove ads" purchase.

The app requests no permission for location, camera, microphone, contacts or phone, and it does not request Android's "read all photos and videos" media permissions: you pick items yourself.

6. Third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising) and the User Messaging Platform (consent)

Carovio is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home and template screens, and occasional full-screen ads when you return to the home screen after an editing session (never during your first session, at least ten minutes apart and at most two a day). Ads are never shown over the editor, during an export, or in your exported files. Ad requests are set to a maximum content rating suitable for teens and are not marked as directed at children.

To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes information from your device, including the advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location such as country or city can be derived), device model, operating-system version, language, app information, and how you interact with ads. The SDK includes Google Play services components that Google uses for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention; they do not give the app any location permission. Depending on your choices and your region, Google and its advertising partners may use this information to show personalised ads. None of your photos, videos, music, project text or exports is shared with Google or advertisers.

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. If you do not consent, ads are not personalised or not requested, as your choice and the law require. Editing never depends on your choice.
  • You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Privacy choices (shown where a choice is required in your region).
  • On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Buying "Remove ads" stops all ads: no ad is requested after the purchase is verified.

How Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play Billing (the "Remove ads" purchase)

"Remove ads" is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google: I never see your card or payment details, and the app never handles them. The app receives only what it needs to confirm the purchase: the product identifier, the order identifier, the purchase state, the purchase token and Google's signature. The app verifies this signature on your device; there is no Carovio server. After reinstalling, or on a new device, use Settings → Remove ads → Restore purchase with the same Google account. Google processes your purchase under the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Open-source components

The app's editing, rendering and video components (including Flutter and AndroidX Media3) and its bundled fonts work entirely on the device and send nothing anywhere. Their licences are listed in Settings → About → Open-source licenses.

7. Children's privacy

Carovio is a general-purpose creative tool intended for users aged 13 and over (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). It is not directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact me and I will help investigate and address the request, including directing you to the relevant service.

8. Data sharing, sale and retention

I do not sell or rent your personal information. Processing by Google for ads, consent and purchases is described in section 6. Everything the app stores on your device (section 3) is kept until you delete a project in the app, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app; uninstalling removes it all, and none of it is backed up to the cloud by the app. Exported files stay wherever you saved them until you delete them. Advertising data is retained by Google under its own policies; purchase records are retained by Google under its applicable policies and legal obligations.

Support correspondence, and any diagnostic log you send me, is kept for as long as needed to resolve your request and any related purchase dispute or legal obligation, then deleted when no longer needed. You can request its deletion using the contact address below; I may need to retain records where required by law.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Delete your data on the device: delete projects in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Ad choices: Settings → Privacy → Privacy choices (where shown), the advertising ID controls in Android settings, or buying "Remove ads".
  • Notifications: disable them for the app in Android settings; exports still work.
  • Access, correction, deletion or objection regarding data held by Google: through your Google Account. If you have rights under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you can also write to me at the address below and I will respond and point you to the right control. Apart from support correspondence, I hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing else for me to access, export or delete on your behalf.

10. International users

I am based in India. The app itself processes your content only on your device. Information processed by Google for ads and purchases may be handled in countries other than your own, under Google's policies and safeguards. Support email you send me is handled through my email provider and may be processed outside your country.

11. Security

Your projects and media copies are stored in Android's private per-application storage, which other apps cannot read. Data exchanged with Google's services is sent over encrypted connections by Google's own SDKs.
