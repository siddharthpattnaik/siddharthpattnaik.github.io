Privacy Policy for NiosPDF (Android)

Application ID: com.niospdf.app
Last updated: 29 October 2026
Other platforms: iOS · Windows and Mac

NiosPDF ("the app"), made by Siddharth Pattnaik, is a PDF viewer and editor. This policy covers the Flutter Android app, which replaces the earlier MAUI Android app under the same application ID. Document processing and AI inference run on your device. There are no user accounts, ads, analytics SDKs or automatic crash-report uploads. The app does not offer a custom AI server connection or send documents or prompts to a hosted AI service.

1. Information we collect

We do not automatically collect personal information or receive your documents, usage history, AI questions or answers. We do not operate an app backend. Network downloads and services you choose to use are explained below.

Support reports. Settings offers Email and Share log actions for the local crash log. Email opens a draft to support@spattnaik.in with system information and the newest part of the log in the message body; Share log lets you send a copy of the log through an app you choose. You can review the draft and it is sent only if you press Send. An AI answer report can also include your question and the generated answer (section 5). If you contact us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. We use this information to respond and investigate the problem, and retain it only as long as needed for those purposes. Do not include confidential information you do not want to share.

2. Your documents and files

Viewing, editing, merging, splitting, annotating, filling and signing forms, compressing, converting, OCR, translation and AI inference are performed on your device. NiosPDF does not upload your files or their contents to us or to an AI provider.

You control external transfers. Sharing, exporting, printing, opening a web link or sending a support report hands the selected content to the app, print service or destination you choose. A file selected through a cloud-backed Android document provider may be downloaded or synced by that provider, including when you save edits back to it. Those services handle data under their own policies; on-device processing does not prevent a destination you choose from using the network.

3. Data stored on your device

The app keeps data in its private application storage and cache, including:

  • Documents imported, received, scanned or created in the app, and working copies of linked files.
  • A local library database with document names, file paths or provider references, tags, reading positions, page counts and text search indexes; cached thumbnails.
  • Signatures and form details (such as a name, address or email) you choose to save for reuse.
  • Unsaved edits and recovery information used to resume interrupted work.
  • Downloaded AI models and local AI data, such as document passages, search vectors, cached summaries and title or tag suggestions.
  • App settings and preferences, including theme, OCR and translation choices.
  • A local crash log of recent errors and stack traces. The logger removes file paths and document-provider references from recorded error messages and does not intentionally record document content. Review any log before sharing it.

Private app data is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Files saved to shared storage or an external document provider remain there. Android backup is disabled for the app; keep your own copies of important documents. When upgrading from the earlier MAUI Android app, supported library records, saved form details, signatures and preferences are migrated locally; compatible downloaded AI models are reused. No migration data is sent to us.

4. Permissions and system access

  • Internet — used to download AI models you select. Google components also need network access for scanner components and OCR or translation models (sections 5 and 6). AI inference does not require a network connection after the required models are downloaded.
  • Scanning and pictures — scanning uses Google Play services' document scanner, which manages camera access in its own interface. NiosPDF does not request a direct camera permission. Pictures are selected through Android's photo or file picker, without broad access to your photo library.
  • Foreground service and notifications — show progress for long operations, such as batch OCR or model downloads, while the app is in the background. Android may ask permission to show notifications; refusing it hides the progress notification.
  • Files you select — the system picker or share sheet grants access to chosen documents. The app may retain a provider grant to reopen a document and save edits back to it. It does not request unrestricted access to all files on your device.

5. AI assistant (optional, local only)

The AI assistant is off by default. You choose whether to enable it and which chat or smart document search models to download. Summaries, document questions and answers, explanations and suggestions are generated locally. NiosPDF has no custom AI server setting, API-key connection or cloud inference fallback. Your documents, prompts and answers are not sent to us or to model publishers for inference or training.

  • Model downloads. Models are downloaded over HTTPS from Hugging Face and its download infrastructure. The catalogue includes models hosted by Qwen, LM Studio Community, HuggingFaceTB (SmolLM2) and CompendiumLabs (bge-small-en-v1.5). Requests retrieve model files; they contain no document content, prompts, account credentials or app tracking identifier. The download host receives your IP address and ordinary request information under the Hugging Face privacy policy. Downloads use storage and may incur your network provider's data charges.
  • Local processing and storage. Downloaded models run on your device using llama.cpp. Document passages, search vectors and cached insights used by the assistant are kept locally. Model availability and speed depend on your device's memory and storage.
  • Reporting an answer. "Report this answer" opens an email draft to support@spattnaik.in containing the question and answer or summary, model name, and app and system information. It does not attach the PDF, but the question or generated answer may contain information from it. Review or remove anything sensitive before sending. Nothing is sent automatically. If no mail app opens, the report text is copied to the clipboard so you can paste it into a message yourself.
  • Control and deletion. You can turn off the assistant and remove downloaded models in Settings → AI assistant. Turning it off does not itself erase stored models or AI data. Removing a document from the library removes its associated AI index and cached insights. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the app's private local data.

6. Third-party components and services

Google ML Kit and Google Play services. Document scanning, text recognition and translation run on-device. Images, recognised text and translation input and output are not sent to Google for processing. Required scanner components, OCR script models and translation language models may be downloaded. NiosPDF disables the Google data-transport upload backend used for ML Kit usage logs in the app process. Google Play services may still handle its own operational and diagnostic information under Google's policies. See the Google ML Kit terms and privacy information and Google Privacy Policy.

App updates. NiosPDF asks the Play Store whether an update is available through Google Play's in-app update library. No document content is included. If you accept an update, Google Play downloads and installs it under its own policies.

Read aloud. Selected document text is handed to your Android text-to-speech engine. Whether that engine uses offline voices or a network service depends on the engine and voice you select and its settings and privacy policy.

Local libraries. The Flutter app uses pdfrx/PDFium, pdf_document, SQLite, local document-conversion libraries, Syncfusion's Flutter signature pad and llama.cpp. These components process documents locally. Their licences and notices are available in the app. The Android app no longer uses the earlier MAUI PDF stack.

External links and optional support payments. Links open in your browser. Optional contributions are handled on Razorpay or PayPal's websites, under their own terms and privacy policies. NiosPDF does not handle your payment credentials. See the Terms of Use.

7. Children's privacy

NiosPDF is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information in a support message, contact us so we can delete it.

8. Data sharing, sale and retention

We do not sell or rent personal data, or share it for advertising. We hold no server-side copy of your documents or AI activity. Voluntary support messages are retained as described in section 1; your email provider and any service you choose have their own retention policies. Local app data remains until removed through the app, cleared with Android's storage controls or removed by uninstalling. Temporary caches may be cleared earlier.

9. Your control and rights

You can remove library entries, saved signatures and form details using the app's controls. Removing a library entry does not delete the source file unless you also choose the file-deletion option. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes private app data but does not delete copies you exported, shared or saved elsewhere. Contact us to request access to or deletion of personal information you sent us voluntarily; we cannot access or erase data held only on your device or by a separate service.

10. Security

The app uses Android's private application storage. Saved form profile details are encrypted with a key protected through Android Keystore. This does not mean that every document, signature, index or cache is separately encrypted by NiosPDF. Protect your device and any exported files, and use PDF password protection where appropriate. No storage or security measure is infallible.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy, support reports or your privacy rights can be sent to sidd.pattnaik@gmail.com.
