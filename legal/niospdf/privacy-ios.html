Privacy Policy for NiosPDF (iOS and iPadOS)

Bundle identifier: com.niospdf.app
Last updated: 24 November 2026
Other platforms: Android · Windows and Mac

NiosPDF ("the app"), made by Siddharth Pattnaik, is a PDF viewer and editor for iPhone and iPad. Document processing and AI inference run on your device. There are no user accounts, ads, analytics SDKs, tracking or automatic crash-report uploads. The app does not send documents or prompts to a hosted AI service.

1. Information we collect

We do not automatically collect personal information or receive your documents, usage history, AI questions or answers. We do not operate an app backend. Network downloads and services you choose to use are explained below.

Support reports. Settings offers Email and Share log actions for the local crash log, and a diagnostics screen in Settings can share an activity log (section 3). Email opens a draft to support@spattnaik.in with system information and the newest part of the log in the message body; Share sends a copy through an app you choose. You can review the draft, and it is sent only if you press Send. An AI answer report can also include your question and the generated answer (section 5). If you contact us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. We use this information to respond and investigate the problem, and keep it only as long as needed for those purposes. Do not include confidential information you do not want to share.

2. Your documents and files

Viewing, editing, merging, splitting, annotating, filling and signing forms, compressing, converting, text recognition, translation and AI inference are performed on your device. NiosPDF does not upload your files or their contents to us or to an AI provider.

You control external transfers. Sharing, exporting, printing, opening a web link or sending a support report hands the selected content to the app, print service or destination you choose. A file you open in place from iCloud Drive or another Files location may be downloaded or synced by that provider, including when you save edits back to it. Those services handle data under their own policies.

3. Data stored on your device

The app keeps data inside its own iOS container, including:

  • Documents imported, received, scanned or created in the app. These are kept in the app's Documents folder, which you can also see in the Files app under On My iPhone or On My iPad → NiosPDF.
  • Working copies of documents you open in place from Files, and cached thumbnails.
  • A local library database with document names, file locations, tags, reading positions, page counts and text search indexes.
  • Signatures and form details (such as a name, address or email) you choose to save for reuse.
  • Unsaved edits and recovery information used to resume interrupted work.
  • Downloaded AI models and local AI data, such as document passages, search vectors, cached summaries and title or tag suggestions.
  • App settings and preferences, stored in the system user-defaults database.
  • A crash log of recent errors and stack traces, and an activity log of what the app recorded while it ran (for example launches, timings and handled errors), kept for troubleshooting. Both remove file paths and file references from what they record and do not intentionally record document content. They stay on your device unless you share or email them. Review any log before sending it.

Deleting the app removes its container. If iCloud Backup or a computer backup is on, iOS may include the app's documents and data in your device backup, handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we have no access to it. Downloaded AI models and caches are excluded from backup. Saved form details are encrypted with a key that stays on this device, so a backup restored to a different device cannot read them.

4. Permissions and system access

iOS asks for your consent before granting the camera, and the rest of the app works without it:

  • Camera — used only when you scan paper with the document camera (Apple's VisionKit). Scanned pages are saved in the app and not transmitted.
  • Pictures — chosen through Apple's photo picker, which gives the app only the pictures you pick, without access to the rest of your photo library.
  • Files you select — the Files picker or share sheet grants access to the documents you choose. The app may keep that access to reopen a document and save edits back to it. It has no access to the rest of your files.
  • Network — used only for downloads you start: AI models (section 5) and, through iOS, translation languages (section 6). Document work needs no connection.

5. AI assistant (optional, local only)

The AI assistant is off by default. You choose whether to enable it and which chat or smart document search models to download. Summaries, document questions and answers, explanations and suggestions are generated locally. NiosPDF has no custom AI server setting, API-key connection or cloud inference fallback. Your documents, prompts and answers are not sent to us or to model publishers for inference or training.

  • Model downloads. Models are downloaded over HTTPS from Hugging Face and its download infrastructure, using iOS's background download service so a download can finish while the app is closed. The catalogue includes models hosted by Qwen, LM Studio Community, HuggingFaceTB (SmolLM2) and CompendiumLabs (bge-small-en-v1.5). Requests retrieve model files; they contain no document content, prompts, account credentials or app tracking identifier. The download host receives your IP address and ordinary request information under the Hugging Face privacy policy. Downloads use storage and may incur your network provider's data charges.
  • Local processing and storage. Downloaded models run on your device using llama.cpp. Document passages, search vectors and cached insights used by the assistant are kept locally. Model availability and speed depend on your device's memory and storage.
  • Reporting an answer. "Report this answer" opens an email draft to support@spattnaik.in containing the question and answer or summary, model name, and app and system information. It does not attach the PDF, but the question or generated answer may contain information from it. Review or remove anything sensitive before sending. Nothing is sent automatically. If no mail app opens, the report text is copied to the clipboard so you can paste it into a message yourself.
  • Control and deletion. You can turn off the assistant and remove downloaded models in Settings → AI assistant. Turning it off does not itself erase stored models or AI data. Removing a document from the library removes its associated AI index and cached insights. Deleting the app removes its local data.

6. Apple frameworks and other components

Text recognition, scanning and translation. Text recognition uses Apple's Vision framework, scanning uses Apple's VisionKit document camera, and translation (iOS 18 and later) uses Apple's Translation framework. All three run on your device; page images, recognised text and translation input and output are not sent to us. Translation languages are downloaded and managed by iOS when you ask for them, under Apple's privacy policy. NiosPDF does not use Google ML Kit or any Google service on iOS.

Read aloud. Selected document text is handed to iOS's speech synthesis, which uses the voices installed on your device.

Local libraries. The app uses pdfrx/PDFium, pdf_document, qpdf, SQLite, local document-conversion libraries, Syncfusion's Flutter signature pad and llama.cpp. These components process documents locally and make no network connections of their own. Their licences and notices are in the app under Settings → About → Open-source licences.

External links and optional support payments. Links open in your browser. Optional contributions are handled on Razorpay or PayPal's websites, under their own terms and privacy policies. NiosPDF does not handle your payment credentials. See the Terms of Use.

7. App Store and TestFlight

The App Store and TestFlight operate their own account, purchase and diagnostic systems, governed by Apple's privacy policy. If you consent to share diagnostics with developers in iOS Settings, or send feedback through TestFlight, Apple may provide us with crash reports and the feedback you send; these come from Apple, not from code in the app.

8. Children's privacy

NiosPDF is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information in a support message, contact us so we can delete it.

9. Data sharing, sale and retention

We do not sell or rent personal data, or share it for advertising. We hold no server-side copy of your documents or AI activity. Voluntary support messages are retained as described in section 1; your email provider and any service you choose have their own retention policies. Local app data remains until you remove it in the app or delete the app. Temporary caches may be cleared earlier, by the app or by iOS.

10. Your control and rights

You can remove library entries, saved signatures and form details using the app's controls. Removing a library entry does not delete the source file unless you also choose to delete the file. Deleting the app removes its private data but does not delete copies you exported, shared or saved elsewhere. Contact us to request access to or deletion of personal information you sent us voluntarily; we cannot access or erase data held only on your device or by a separate service.

11. Security

App data is stored in the app's iOS container, which other apps cannot read, and is protected by your device passcode and iOS data protection. Saved form details are additionally encrypted with a key kept in the iOS Keychain. Passwords for protected PDFs are used to open the document and are not saved. This does not mean that every document, signature, index or cache is separately encrypted by NiosPDF. Protect your device and any exported files, and use PDF password protection where appropriate. No storage or security measure is infallible.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy, support reports or your privacy rights can be sent to sidd.pattnaik@gmail.com.
