Privacy Policy for Notification Domino (Android)

Application ID: in.spattnaik.notificationdomino (Android)
Last updated: 27 November 2026
See also: Terms of Use

Notification Domino ("the game") is a puzzle game made by Siddharth Pattnaik ("I", "me"), in which you send pretend in-game notifications to cartoon characters and watch the chain reaction. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with your information. In short: the game has no account and I run no servers. Your progress is kept only on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID, and sells one optional item (Remove Ads) through Google Play.

The "notifications" in the game are fictional cards that exist only inside the game. The game does not read, send or post notifications on your phone, and it does not access your real messages, contacts or apps.

1. Information I collect

The game does not send your gameplay data to servers operated by me. The game has no sign-up, no in-game chat, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK of mine, and it does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.

Third-party services process information as described in section 4. Their developer reports may include advertising, sales and diagnostic information. If you contact me for support, I receive your email address, your message and any information you choose to include, such as an order number or screenshot. I use this correspondence to answer your request and resolve problems. Please do not send passwords or full payment-card details.

2. Data stored on your device

The game keeps a small save file (and a backup copy of it) in its own app storage so that it can work between sessions. It is removed when you reset progress in Settings, clear the game's storage or uninstall the game. It contains:

  • your progress: levels solved, stars, unlocked worlds and achievements;
  • your coin balance and which rewards have already been paid out;
  • whether you own Remove Ads, so the game knows not to show ads between levels;
  • your settings: music and sound volume, vibration, language, playback speed, accessibility options (reduced motion, larger text, colour-blind-safe mode) and which tutorials you have seen.

The save file is not uploaded anywhere. There is no cloud save, so progress does not move to a new device.

3. Permissions the game requests, and why

  • Vibration — short haptic feedback when a card is sent and a chain reaction happens. It can be switched off in Settings.
  • Internet and network state — to load ads and to process purchases through Google Play. The game itself plays fully offline; without a connection there are simply no ads.
  • Advertising ID — added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK so that ads can be shown and measured (see section 4).
  • Billing — added by Google Play Billing so that the optional purchase can be made.
  • Keep awake and foreground service — added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which uses Android's background-work scheduler to finish ad-related tasks reliably. The game itself does not run in the background, keep your screen on, or show a persistent notification.

The game requests no runtime permissions: no notifications, location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or phone access.

4. Third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising)

The game shows two kinds of ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK: rewarded ads, which play only when you choose to watch one for a reward (for example a hint or an extra move), and occasional full-screen ads on the results screen between levels. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google collects and processes information from your device, including the advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location such as country or city can be derived), device model, operating system, language, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information about errors in the ads SDK. Depending on your choices and your region, Google and its advertising partners may use this to show personalised ads.

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time with the Ad privacy option in Settings.
  • On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Buying Remove Ads removes the ads between levels. Rewarded ads remain available because they only ever play when you ask for one.

How Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play Billing (in-app purchase)

The optional Remove Ads purchase is made through Google Play, using the Unity In-App Purchasing library. Payment is handled entirely by Google: I never see your card or payment details. The game receives only what it needs to deliver the item: the product identifier, the order identifier and a purchase token.

Unity

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Analytics, Unity Ads and Unity cloud crash reporting are switched off in this game.

5. Children's privacy

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact me and I will help investigate and address the request, including directing you to the relevant service.

6. Data sharing, sale and retention

I do not sell or rent your personal information. Third-party processing for ads and purchases is described in section 4. The save file on your device is kept until you reset progress, clear the game's storage or uninstall it. Advertising data is retained by Google under its own policies; purchase records are retained by Google under its applicable policies and legal obligations.

Support correspondence is kept for as long as needed to resolve your request and any related purchase dispute or legal obligation, then deleted when no longer needed. You can request its deletion using the contact address below; I may need to retain records where required by law.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Delete your data on the device: use Reset progress in Settings, clear the game's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the game.
  • Ad choices: the Ad privacy option in Settings (where shown), and the advertising ID controls in Android settings.
  • Access, correction, deletion or objection regarding data held by Google: through your Google Account. If you have rights under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you can also write to me at the address below and I will respond and point you to the right control. For support correspondence held by me, contact me directly.

8. Security

The save file is stored in Android's per-application storage, which other apps cannot read. Data exchanged with Google's services is sent over encrypted connections by Google's own SDKs.
